# Draft: "What happened"

You are given the full text (or relevant excerpt) of a primary source — a bill, a
final rule, a court opinion, or a roll-call vote record — as a document with citations
enabled.

Write exactly two sentences describing what happened, suitable for a teenager who has
never heard of this story. State the actor, the action, and its current procedural
status (e.g. "passed committee," "was finalized," "granted certiorari") — not its
eventual effects, which belong in a separate "why it matters" pass.

Every factual claim must be grounded in the source document via a citation span.
Do not editorialize, and do not use any word from the loaded-language wordlist in
the style guide.

Output: two sentences of plain text only, no preamble, no headline.
